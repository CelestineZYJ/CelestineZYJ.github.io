About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Computer Science Department at the College of Staten Island, City University of New York.

Before joining the City University of New York, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, where I was advised by Prof. Heng Ji and Prof. Chengxiang Zhai. I also worked with Prof. H. Chad Lane.

My research focuses on reliable and interpretable AI, spanning knowledge, reasoning, and autonomous agents.

Research Interest

My research focuses on reliable and interpretable AI, particularly the relationship between knowledge, reasoning, and intelligent behavior.

A fundamental question motivating my research is: What stands between what AI systems know and what they can reliably do? My work explores this question through knowledge overshadowing and updating, interpretable and verifiable reasoning, and reliable autonomous agents.

Beyond AI systems themselves, I am interested in the reciprocal relationship between AI and human intelligence: how AI shapes human understanding and judgment, and how human reasoning and oversight contribute to AI reliability. My long-term vision is Reliable AI for Human Flourishing, where advances in AI and human capabilities reinforce one another.
